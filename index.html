Bura

Wind over the Croatian Adriatic, drawn as tens of thousands of particles flowing along the current wind field. Colour shows speed. The fading trails show direction.

Reading it

  • Bura (NE) comes down off the Velebit and Biokovo in narrow, gusty jets, strongest below Senj, Karlobag and Makarska.
  • Jugo (SE) is a broad, steady flow along the length of the Adriatic, strongest in the open south.
  • Drag the timeline for the last 24 h and the next 48 h. Hover or tap the map for local wind. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Data

  • Observations: DHMZ station reports (hourly), Open Licence of the Republic of Croatia.
  • Model: Open-Meteo 10 m wind and gusts (ICON and other national models), CC BY 4.0.
  • Coastline: Natural Earth, public domain.

Station reports are blended into the model by inverse-distance weighting of observed-minus-model wind vectors. Their influence fades with distance and, in the forecast, with lead time. This is a visualisation, not a navigational forecast. For safety at sea, use meteo.hr.
